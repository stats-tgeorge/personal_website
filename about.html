Tyler George

I am an Assistant Professor of Statistics (and data science) at Cornell College - a small undergraduate liberal arts college. I received my PhD in Statistics and Analytics at Central Michigan University. During my PhD I also studied mathematics and statistics education. My dissertation work involved creating a new lack of fit test for linear regression models. My interests are broadly in statistics education and data science.
